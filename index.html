Where do the words go? Nowhere.

Otterly Me! has no backend. There are no servers holding your child's journal, because there is nowhere for it to go. Every entry is saved in the web browser's own storage, right on your family's device — each child's journal kept separate under its own key.

The app talks to no one. No analytics, no trackers, no ads, no accounts, nothing uploaded — ever. The only things it ever downloads are the page itself and its fonts. Once it's loaded, it even works offline.

No sharing, no friends, no comments, no feed. The only person who can read your child's pages is someone holding the device — exactly like the paper diary under their pillow.
